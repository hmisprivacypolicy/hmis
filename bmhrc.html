Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of information handled through the BMHRC HMIS Mobile App.

BMHRC HMIS is a hospital information management application developed and implemented by C-DAC for Bhopal Memorial Hospital and Research Centre (BMHRC) and its associated healthcare services and facilities.

This Privacy Policy explains how the BMHRC HMIS Mobile App accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application and its healthcare-related services.

Information We Collect and Use

The BMHRC HMIS Mobile App may access information required to provide hospital and healthcare-related services. The information accessed or processed depends on the features used by the user.

Where applicable, information may include patient-related information, appointment or hospital service information, prescriptions, documents, and other information required to provide the requested HMIS services.

We use such information only for legitimate healthcare, hospital administration, service delivery, authentication, communication, and other purposes necessary to provide the functionality of the application.

Camera and Microphone Permissions

The application may request access to the device camera and microphone when required for features such as tele-consultation or video/audio communication between patients and authorized healthcare professionals.

Camera and microphone access is used only when required by the relevant application functionality. Users can manage or revoke these permissions through the Android device settings.

Files and Documents

The application may access device files or documents when required to download, save, open, or view prescriptions, medical documents, reports, or other documents provided through HMIS services.

File access is used only for the requested application functionality. Users can manage applicable permissions through the Android device settings.

Use of Information

Information processed through BMHRC HMIS may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing hospital and healthcare-related services.
  • Managing appointments and hospital interactions.
  • Supporting tele-consultation and communication features.
  • Providing prescriptions, reports, documents, and other requested services.
  • Authenticating and authorizing users where applicable.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the application.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of HMIS services.
  • Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requirements.
Sharing of Information

C-DAC does not sell or use personal or sensitive user information for advertising purposes.

Information is not shared with third parties for their independent marketing or advertising purposes. Information may, however, be processed or disclosed when necessary to provide the requested BMHRC HMIS services, by authorized personnel or service providers acting on behalf of the hospital/project, or when required by applicable law or a lawful governmental request.

Security of Your Information

C-DAC respects the privacy of individuals using BMHRC HMIS services and takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Where information is transmitted between the application and backend services, appropriate security measures are used to protect the transmission of information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant healthcare and hospital services, meet operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or institutional requirements.

Where applicable, users may request information regarding deletion or handling of their personal information by contacting the BMHRC HMIS support team using the contact details provided below. Requests may be subject to applicable legal, medical-record retention, and institutional requirements.

Children's Privacy

BMHRC HMIS is intended to provide healthcare and hospital-related services. The application does not knowingly use personal information from children for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where healthcare services are provided to a minor, information may be processed as part of the relevant healthcare service in accordance with applicable laws and institutional procedures.

Third-Party Services

The BMHRC HMIS Mobile App may use technical infrastructure or services required to operate the application and provide HMIS functionality. Where third-party services are used, information is handled in accordance with applicable contractual, security, and privacy requirements.

Third-party services are not authorized to use personal or sensitive information for purposes unrelated to the provision of the requested services.

Permissions

The application requests device permissions only when they are required for specific functionality. Depending on the features available in the application, these permissions may include:

  • Camera: Used for supported video consultation or related features.
  • Microphone: Used for supported audio/video consultation features.
  • Files/Documents: Used to save, open, or view prescriptions and documents.

Users can manage available permissions through the Android device settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

C-DAC may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the BMHRC HMIS Mobile App, healthcare services, applicable requirements, or user feedback.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised. Users are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to remain informed about how information is handled.
