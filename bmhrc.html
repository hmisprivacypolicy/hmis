Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BMHRC HMIS mobile application ("App"), developed and maintained by Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) for Bhopal Memorial Hospital and Research Centre (BMHRC). This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a comprehensive healthcare service, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, age, gender, contact details (phone number, email address), and demographic information.
  • Health and Medical Information: Patient IDs (UHID/CRNO), medical history, appointment details, test reports, prescriptions, and other health-related data necessary for hospital services.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and App version for performance optimization and bug tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly used to facilitate and improve healthcare delivery. We use your data to:

  • Enable online appointment booking and registration.
  • Provide access to lab reports, prescriptions, and medical records.
  • Send SMS or push notifications regarding appointment confirmations, hospital updates, and reports.
  • Improve the functionality, security, and user experience of the App.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or medical information to third parties. Your data is only shared in the following circumstances:

  • Authorized Healthcare Providers: Your medical information is accessible to authorized doctors and staff at BMHRC for the purpose of your treatment and care.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or government regulations.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your medical data. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or mobile device is 100% secure.

5. Permissions Required by the App

To function properly, the App may request the following permissions:

  • Internet/Network State: To connect to the hospital servers to fetch data.
  • Storage (Read/Write): To download and save PDF lab reports or prescriptions to your device.
  • Camera (Optional): Required only if profile picture updates or document scanning features are utilized.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal and medical information associated with your account. If you need to update or correct your demographic data, please contact the hospital registration desk, as critical medical records cannot be altered directly through the App for legal reasons.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
